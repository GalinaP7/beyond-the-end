# Beyond the End — Gameplay Rules

Beyond the End is built around rewarding accomplishments, participation, and contributions to the world.

These rules define how progression is intended to work and help keep Story Points and rewards fair.

---

## ⭐ Story Points

- Story Points represent permanent lifetime progression.
- SP cannot be traded or transferred between players.
- SP is not spent when Story Tokens are used.
- AFK farming does not earn SP.
- Repeating the same action does not repeatedly earn SP unless a quest, challenge, or event specifically allows it.

---

## 🪙 Story Tokens

- Story Tokens are earned through Story Point progression and other approved rewards.
- Tokens may not be duplicated or generated through exploits.
- Exploiting bugs or loopholes to obtain additional Tokens is not considered valid progression.

---

## ⚔ Quests & Achievements

- Quests should be claimed before completion unless stated otherwise.
- Achievements can only be rewarded once unless specifically marked as repeatable.
- Rewards are granted for completing the actual objective, not for exploiting technicalities in its wording.

---

## 🏗 Community Projects

Community projects allow players to earn progression by contributing to larger shared goals.

Claim a project before beginning work on it.

Story Point rewards may depend on each player's contribution rather than being divided equally automatically.

---

## 📬 Community Decisions

Servers may use suggestions and voting to make decisions about quests, events, projects, rules, rewards, or other shared systems.

When a decision is put to a general vote:

> **Majority vote wins.**

Server owners may choose how suggestions and voting are organized for their community.

---

## ✦ Fair Play

Beyond the End is meant to create new reasons to play Minecraft—not new ways to grind the progression system.

Bugs, exploits, loopholes, duplication, or other unintended mechanics should not be used to gain Story Points, Story Tokens, titles, achievements, or other progression rewards.

When something falls outside the written rules, server administrators have final judgment over whether it counts as legitimate progression.

---

## 🌎 Make It Your Own

These rules reflect the original gameplay structure Beyond the End was designed around, but they are **not requirements for every server**.

Beyond the End is meant to provide a framework that server owners can adapt to their own world and community. You are free to change progression rules, Token restrictions, quest requirements, voting systems, community project rules, or anything else to better fit how your server plays.

Use these rules as a starting point—not a limitation.

**Every world tells a different story.**
