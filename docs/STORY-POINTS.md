# Beyond the End — Story Points & Progression

Story Points are the core progression system of Beyond the End.

Earn Story Points by participating in the world, reach new ranks, and earn Story Tokens to spend on rewards.

```text
Activities & Accomplishments
          ↓
     Story Points
          ↓
   ┌──────┴──────┐
   ↓             ↓
Ranks       Story Tokens
```

---

# ⭐ Story Points

**Story Points (SP)** represent your permanent lifetime progression.

SP can be awarded for accomplishments such as quests, achievements, events, community projects, and other contributions to the world.

Story Points are **not currency** and are not spent.

If you have:

```text
150 SP
```

you keep that 150 SP even after spending your Story Tokens.

---

# 🪙 Story Tokens

Story Tokens are the physical, spendable currency of Beyond the End.

You earn:

> **1 Story Token for every 10 lifetime Story Points reached.**

| Lifetime SP | Total Tokens Earned |
|---:|---:|
| 10 | 1 |
| 50 | 5 |
| 100 | 10 |
| 500 | 50 |
| 1,000 | 100 |

Tokens are real Minecraft items that can be stored in your inventory or containers and used for Beyond the End rewards and other systems.

Token rewards are based on your **total lifetime SP**, so crossing multiple 10-SP milestones at once awards each milestone you reached.

# ⚔ Ranks

Ranks mark major progression milestones and are automatically determined by lifetime Story Points.

| Story Points | Rank |
|---:|---|
| 0–99 | Unranked |
| 100–299 | Wanderer |
| 300–499 | Adventurer |
| 500–999 | Explorer |
| 1,000–1,999 | Pathfinder |
| 2,000–2,999 | Hero |
| 3,000–4,999 | Champion |
| 5,000–9,999 | Legend |
| 10,000+ | Mythic |

Reaching a new rank is announced to the server.

# ⚔ Titles

Titles are separate from ranks.

**Ranks** are automatically earned through Story Points.

**Titles** are special accomplishments that can be unlocked and equipped.

For example:

```text
PlayerName [Frogleaper]
Wanderer ✦ 150 SP
```

A player can unlock multiple titles but equip only one at a time.
Equipped title appears beside the player's username.

# Progression at a Glance

```text
Complete activities
        ↓
Earn Story Points
        ↓
Reach Token milestones
        ↓
Earn Story Tokens
        ↓
Reach major SP milestones
        ↓
Rank up
```

Story Points record **how far you've progressed**.

Story Tokens are **what you can spend**.

Ranks show **major progression milestones**.

Titles show **what you've accomplished**.
