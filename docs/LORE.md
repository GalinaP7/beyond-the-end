# Beyond the End — Lore

Beyond the End begins where Minecraft's traditional progression ends.

The Ender Dragon is defeated. Elytra have been found. Bases have been built. The world has already lived through its first adventures.

**Now the real story begins.**

---

## 📖 Chapter I — THE END?

*If you are reading this, you have crossed the Nether.*

*You have defeated the Ender Dragon.*

*You have taken to the skies with Elytra.*

*You have built homes, raised beacons, gathered riches, and conquered nearly everything this world was supposed to offer.*

*So there is only one question left.*

***What now?***

*Defeating the Ender Dragon wasn't the story's finale, it was only the beginning.*

***It was the prologue.***

---

## 📚 The Chronicle

The Chronicle records the history created as the world continues.

```text
WORLD CHRONICLE
│
├── YEAR I
│   ├── Chapter I — The End?
│   ├── Chapter II — ???
│   └── ...
│
├── PLAYER HISTORIES
├── LEGENDARY EVENTS
├── RELICS
└── RECORDS & MEMORIALS
```

Future chapters can grow from quests, discoveries, community projects, events, and whatever else happens along the way.

---

## 🌎 Your Story

Chapter I provides the starting point. Everything after it can be adapted to fit each world.

Server owners can create their own chapters, lore, events, locations, and Chronicle entries.

**Every world tells a different story.**
